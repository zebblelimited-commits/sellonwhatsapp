SellOnWhatsApp Referral Program
Core concept

Invite → Earn Points → Help Users Grow → Redeem Cash

Every user gets a unique referral link/code:

sellonwhatsapp.com/join?ref=MAXJHON

or

SellOnWhatsApp Referral Code: MAXJHON

Points accumulate in the user's Referral Wallet and become redeemable once they reach ₦5,000 worth of points.

1. Point Value

I recommend keeping the system extremely simple:

100 points = ₦100

Therefore:

Points	Value
100	₦100
500	₦500
1,000	₦1,000
2,500	₦2,500
5,000	₦5,000 redeemable
10,000	₦10,000
20,000	₦20,000

This makes the wallet very easy for users to understand.

2. Referral Actions

I'd split rewards into Onboarding Rewards, Growth Rewards, and Transaction Rewards.

A. Buyer Referral

When someone successfully refers a buyer:

600 points = ₦600

This directly produces your desired:

10 buyers = ₦6,000

But don't award all 600 points simply when someone creates an account.

Instead:

Buyer referral lifecycle

Step 1 — Sign up

0 points

The person creates an account using the referral link.

Step 2 — Complete profile

+100 points

Step 3 — First meaningful marketplace activity

For example:

browses products
saves a product
adds product to cart
contacts seller

+100 points

Step 4 — First successful order

+400 points

Total:

600 points

This makes the referral worth ₦600.

That means:

10 successful buyer referrals = 6,000 points = ₦6,000

3. Seller Referral

This should be more valuable because a seller can potentially generate many transactions.

1,000 points = ₦1,000

Your desired milestone becomes:

10 successful sellers = ₦10,000

But again, don't give the full ₦1,000 for merely opening an account.

I'd make the seller referral progressive:

Seller action	Referrer earns
Seller registers	100 pts
Completes profile/store	200 pts
Adds first 5 products	200 pts
Publishes store	100 pts
Gets first successful order	400 pts
Total	1,000 pts

This is much better than:

"Invite seller → ₦1,000"

because you're paying for an activated seller, not an email address.

5. Important: Separate "Referral Points" From "Activity Points"

I would actually make this distinction in your database.

Referral Points

Points generated because someone you referred performed an action.

Example:

John referred Mary.

Mary creates a store.

John gets referral points.

Personal Growth Points

Points earned by the user themselves.

Example:

John completes his profile.

John gets activity points.

This lets you later build much more sophisticated campaigns.

For example:

Complete your profile this week and earn 200 bonus points.

or:

Add 10 products this weekend and earn 500 bonus points.

18. Don't Give Points for Clicks

This is extremely important.

Don't do:

Share = 20 points
Click = 10 points

Otherwise people will spam WhatsApp groups.

Instead:

Share

No immediate monetary reward.

Click

No monetary reward.

Registration

Small reward.

Activation

Major reward.

Successful transaction

Major reward.

That keeps the economics sustainable.

19. Anti-Fraud Rules

You definitely need this from day one.

A referral should only become "successful" after the referred account passes certain conditions.

For example:

Buyer

Must:

have a unique phone/email
verify account
complete basic profile
not already exist
perform meaningful activity
preferably complete first order
Seller

Must:

create unique account
verify account
complete store profile
add at least 5 products
publish store
preferably complete first successful order

And:

Don't allow:
User A
 ↓
creates 20 fake accounts
 ↓
earns ₦12,000

You can detect:

duplicate phone numbers
duplicate email
same device patterns
suspicious IP patterns
repeated payment instruments
unusually fast account creation
self-referrals
referral loops
20. Don't Immediately Credit Cash

I'd use a pending → approved → available system.

Example:

Referral Reward

+1,000 pts

Status: Pending

After validation:

+1,000 pts

Status: Approved

Then:

Available Balance
₦8,200

This gives you time to reverse fraudulent referrals.

ReferralTransactions
